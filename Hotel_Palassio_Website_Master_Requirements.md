# HOTEL PALASSIO INTERNATIONAL --- Website Master Requirements

## Business Context

-   Hotel: **HOTEL PALASSIO INTERNATIONAL**
-   Address: Plot No. 6/C-921, Sector-6, Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow,
    Uttar Pradesh -- 226010
-   Property: **14 rooms + 2 banquet halls + 1 rooftop**
-   Business ecosystem: Stay + Celebrate + Dine + Food Delivery +
    Corporate + Events
-   Primary goal: build a conversion-focused website that becomes the
    hotel's central digital sales, enquiry and guest-communication
    platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Primary Website Goals

The website must: - Sell rooms/direct bookings. - Generate room
enquiries. - Showcase all room categories. - Generate banquet, wedding
and corporate-event leads. - Showcase the restaurant and rooftop. -
Accept table/reservation enquiries. - Send customers to verified
Swiggy/Zomato listings. - Support future direct food ordering. - Promote
offers and events. - Capture WhatsApp/call/email leads. - Support
customer reviews. - Support Google/local SEO. - Build a consent-based
first-party guest/customer database. - Provide a CMS/admin dashboard. -
Integrate with booking engine/PMS/POS/CRM later.

# 35. Food Compliance

The currently uploaded FSSAI certificate should not automatically be
treated as the final restaurant licence for Hotel Palassio. The document
identifies **THE MULTICUISINE RASOI / SHRADDHA SONI** and classifies the
business as **Hawker (Itinerant / Mobile food vendor)** at the hotel
address. Before publishing the restaurant/rooftop as the hotel's
food-service operation and before delivery-platform onboarding, the
correct food-business licensing/registration setup should be reviewed.

Once the correct authorization is in place, display the applicable FSSAI
number/certificate information in the website footer or food-compliance
section.

FSSAI states that food businesses must be licensed/registered and uses
FoSCoS for the licensing system. citeturn0search0turn0search4

# 37. Service Charge

Do not design the billing/checkout flow to automatically add a mandatory
service charge. The Consumer Affairs Ministry/CCPA has stated that
hotels/restaurants cannot add service charge automatically or by default
and that it is voluntary/optional. citeturn0search9
